## **缓存策略速查表**

| 接口                    | 缓存位置            | TTL   | Key规则                   |
| :-------------------- | :-------------- | :---- | :---------------------- |
| GET /categories       | 内存+localStorage | 30min | `/categories`           |
| GET /communities      | 内存+localStorage | 30min | `/communities`          |
| GET /items            | 内存              | 2min  | `/items?{params}`       |
| GET /forum/posts      | 内存              | 2min  | `/forum/posts?{params}` |
| GET /announcements    | 内存              | 5min  | `/announcements`        |
| GET /items/{id}       | 内存              | 5min  | `/items/{id}`           |
| GET /forum/posts/{id} | 内存              | 5min  | `/forum/posts/{id}`     |
